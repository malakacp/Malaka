I am an **Innovative Textile Engineer and Textile material designer** with over 10 years of R&D experience bridging advanced material science, 3D flatbed knitting, and commercial product engineering. Currently, I serve as **Deputy General Manager – Technology Entrepreneur** at **Twinery (Innovation by MAS)**, where I lead material white-space strategy and commercialize next-generation textile technologies.

My focus is on solving high-barrier engineering challenges—transitioning novel e-textiles, medical knits, and sustainable material architectures from early-stage lab concepts into globally distributed consumer and healthcare products.

---

### 🔬 Core Areas of Expertise

* **Advanced 3D Flatbed Knitting:** Whole-garment knitting, Advance functional knit structures, textile composite material development and programming on Shima Seiki (APEX3) and Stoll (M1 Plus) platforms.
* **Smart & Electronic Textiles (E-Textiles):** Conductive pathways, embedded sensors/actuators, and active heating platforms for wearable electronics/ soft electronics, soft robotics. 
* **Medical & Therapeutic Wearables:** Knitted pneumatic compression sleeves, vibration therapy systems, and knitted respiratory interface components.
* **Sustainable & Circular Engineering:** Circular mono-material structures designed for recyclability, including 3D-knitted solutions engineered to eliminate non-recyclable polyurethane foam.

---

### 🚀 Commercial Impact & Brand Deployments

Over the past decade, I have led technical R&D and manufacturing translation for flagship international brands:

* **Victoria’s Secret & PINK:** Co-developed and commercialized the mono-material 3D-knitted bra cup to replace conventional PU foam, as well as seasonal technical undergarment collections.
* **Calvin Klein:** Developed technical knitted apparel and undergarment collections.
* **Therabody:** Partnered on the technical flat-knit architecture for the vibration therapy glove.
* **ResMed:** Engineered precision flat-knitted medical soft-goods (headgear and mouthpiece components).
* **Soft-Goods for Robotics & Automation:**  Researched and prototyped seamless 3D-knitted enclosures, flexible joint coverings, and conformal textile skins for robotic systems.

---

### 🏆 Recognitions & Intellectual Property

* **10+ International Patents:** Co-inventor across granted and published patents in the US, Europe, Great Britain, and PCT applications covering conductive pathways, 3D actuation structures, fluid absorption, and active heating.
* **ISPO Award Winner (2023):** Recognized for the engineering of a 3D-knitted seamless ergonomic performance boxer with targeted functional zones.
* **CES Innovation Awards (2017):** Technical R&D contributor on award-winning active heating platforms for smart apparel.
