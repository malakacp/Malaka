### 💼 Professional Experience

#### **Deputy General Manager – Technology Entrepreneur**
*Twinery, Innovation by MAS* | **2025 – Present**
* Lead technical white-space exploration, intellectual property generation, and commercialization strategies for advanced textile platforms.
* Direct multidisciplinary teams scaling high-barrier 3D flatbed knitting, smart wearables, and sustainable materials into commercial production.

#### **Manager – R&D in Flatbed Knitting Technology**
*Twinery, Innovation by MAS* | **2021 – 2025**
* Spearheaded the **onEKnit platform** (2017–2025), driving breakthroughs in fine-gauge lightweight structures and 3D fully-fashioned garments.
* Technical lead on the **Victoria’s Secret Forever Bra** closed-loop recyclable pad and the **ISPO Award-Winning 2023** customizable performance boxer brief.
* Invented and co-invented multiple international patents across the US, Europe, and Great Britain.

#### **Assistant Manager – R&D**
*Twinery, Innovation by MAS* | **2018 – 2020**
* Developed medical-grade pneumatic compression systems and vibration therapy architectures partnered with brands like Therabody and ResMed.
* Formulated washable, stretchable conductive yarn pathways for smart electronic wearables.

#### **Executive – Smart Textiles**
*Twinery, Innovation by MAS* | **2016 – 2018**
* Technical contributor on active knitted heating systems recognized with multiple **CES 2017 Innovation Awards**.
