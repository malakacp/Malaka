### The Engineering Challenge
Conventional women's bra cups rely on laminated polyurethane (PU) foam, which is notoriously difficult to recycle and creates a major roadblock for textile circularity.

### The Technical Innovation
* Engineered a **100% mono-material 3D flatbed knitted structure** with variable zonal modulus.
* Designed thickness transitions directly into the knitted matrix to provide structural lift, breathability, and contouring without foam inserts.
* Formulated on automated industrial flatbed knitting platforms (Shima Seiki / Stoll) for zero-waste production.

### Commercial Launch & IP
* **Brand Deployment:** Commercialized and launched with **Victoria’s Secret & PINK**.
* **Patent Protection:** Protected under granted patent **US20230010185A1** (*Fabric with variable fabric properties for sustainable bra structure*).
